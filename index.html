Test both directions on the dopamine cells:

TMS produces a loud "clicking" sound and vibrates the scalp. How will you prove that changes in curiosity are actually caused by brain stimulation rather than just the sound, vibration, or placebo effect?

1. Sham TMS (The Gold Standard): Turn the magnetic coil 90 degrees away from the head or use an inactive sham coil. It makes the exact same loud clicking noise and feels like tapping on the scalp, but no magnetic field enters the brain. If curiosity changes during real TMS but not sham TMS, the brain manipulation caused it!

2. Control Brain Region (The Vertex): Apply active TMS to a completely unrelated control area at the top of the skull (the vertex). Both groups receive real stimulation, but only stimulation to the curiosity region (IFG) should alter curiosity.
